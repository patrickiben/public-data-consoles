Coastal Water Resilience

Live coastal-surge monitoring at NOAA tide gauge 8726520 (St. Petersburg, FL), joined to a hurricane response plan built on the ten-metric diagnostic suite from a contamination-resilient cluster-randomized-trial (CRT) design. Exposed and unexposed lift stations at a surge level play the two trial arms; each metric pairs an analytical and an operational mitigation. The network it runs on is synthetic.

Key findings

MEASURED · real network
  • Exposure is a cliff. A two-foot difference between 6 and 8 ft takes 15 stations to 56.
  • The fixed 6 ft screen understated Helene by 4.6x (15 vs 69). It was in effect calibrated to Idalia (15 vs 15).
  • Milton screened 2 stations, correctly, because it pushed water out of the bay.
  • 72 of 131 stations (55%) are in Zone A, and 52 of Helene's 69 were. So access, not pump capacity, is the binding constraint once Zone A is ordered, which argues for pre-staging and remote monitoring.
  • The criticality flag does not encode surge exposure.

What the backtest says

INTERPRETIVE
  • Helene's biggest failure was the plant, which is outside a lift-station ladder's scope.
  • The reporting unit is the manhole, so no station-level hit rate is possible from public data.
  • Milton released about four times Helene's sewage volume with zero surge. That is the strongest argument for treating grid power and rainfall inflow as first-class hazards, and against a surge-only console.

Method

INTERPRETIVE

The console applies the ten-metric diagnostic suite from a contamination-resilient cluster-randomized-trial (CRT) design to a wastewater lift-station network. At a given surge level the stations at or below the flood plane are one arm (exposed) and the rest are the other (unexposed). In a trial, contamination between arms biases the estimate; in a sewer, failure propagates across force-main connections, so the same geometry that governs contamination bias in a trial governs cascade in a network.

The suite has three pillars: Statistical Interchangeability (metrics 1 to 3), Topological and Spatial Cohesion (4 to 7) and Contamination Resilience (8 to 10). Each metric has a threshold; when it is crossed, the console shows the sub-optimal condition, an analytical mitigation (how to read the numbers) and an operational mitigation (what to do in the field). The response plan collects the operational mitigations of the sub-optimal metrics into timeline phases.

Live feeds set context only: the gauge reading and the OFS forecast map onto the ladder, and the NHC track suggests a phase. Nothing here predicts which station fails.

How the synthetic network was calibrated

MODELED · computed liveMEASURED · real aggregates

The network is generated in your browser from a fixed seed (mulberry32, seed 20260904), so every visitor sees the same 130 stations. Ground elevations are drawn inside bands whose counts reproduce the real network's surge ladder and storm planes exactly, with a few fixed values pinning the published quantiles. Stations sit on a fictional peninsula in a local kilometer frame (barrier islands and keys, causeways, tidal creeks, an inland ridge): lower stations on islands, shorelines and creek lowlands, higher ones inland. The 15-edge force-main topology, evacuation zones and criticality flags are set to match the real network's aggregates. The three treatment plants are the terminals: each force-main sink links, schematically, to its basin's plant (these links are not counted as edges). The real network lists 131 stations; the published calibration aggregates (elevation ladder, zone split) cover 130 of them, so the synthetic network has 130. What is withheld, and why

INTERPRETIVE

This public version withholds every real station name, coordinate, facility ID and per-station value (elevation, zone, topology, criticality). A map of which wastewater stations sit lowest, which are choke points and which back up first is a map of critical infrastructure; it belongs with the utility, not on a public page. What remains is what is already public or aggregate: NOAA gauge readings, storm-level backtest counts, publicly reported sewage volumes, and network-level summaries (counts, shares, the ten metric values). The synthetic IDs (LS-001 to LS-130) and names are neutral and do not correspond to real facilities.

Data sources

MEASURED · public feeds
  • NOAA CO-OPS station 8726520, St. Petersburg, Tampa Bay: observed water level (6-minute), tide predictions, and the Operational Forecast System (OFS) water-level guidance, via the CO-OPS Data API.
  • NOAA National Hurricane Center forecast and observed positions, via the Esri Living Atlas Active Hurricanes feature service.
  • National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov), active alerts at the gauge point.
  • Calibration aggregates: the real network's ground elevations from USGS 3DEP, evacuation zones and force-main topology, summarized at network level only (real network, 2026-09-04).
  • Backtest volumes: FDEP Public Notice of Pollution, City of St. Petersburg disclosures, and press reports quoting the City.

All feeds are keyless and fetched by your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.

Caveats

INTERPRETIVE
  • Ground elevation is not wet-well or panel elevation. The real calibration uses USGS 3DEP ground elevation, so the ladder tiers are screening flags, not flood lines.
  • Topology direction is inferred from elevation (force mains point downhill), not traced from as-builts.
  • The gauge is one point. Surge varies across a bay; a single gauge reading stands in for the whole network.
  • Surge is one hazard. Grid power and rainfall inflow drove Milton's releases; this console does not model them.
  • Feeds can lag or drop. Every panel shows its feed state and timestamp; a stale or unavailable feed is labeled as such.

Utility hook

MODELED · empty by default

The script defines STATUS_OVERRIDE = {}. A utility could wire it to SCADA: keys are station IDs and values look like {status: 'down' | 'surcharged' | 'ok', note: '...'}. An overridden station is drawn with a cross on the map and its status appears in the station panel. In this public copy it is empty, so nothing is overridden.
