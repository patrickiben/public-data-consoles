River Flood Propagation

Rivers are a directed network — water flows one way, and an upstream rise leads downstream flooding by hours to days. This walks the main‑stem gauge chain, identifies elevated upstream gauges and compares downstream forecast peak times — instead of only reacting to wherever the water is loudest right now.

How this works

About. An unofficial prototype that reorganizes NOAA's own public gauge observations & forecasts into the directed river network. It does not forecast better than NWS — it surfaces the propagation: upstream/downstream order and independently reported forecast peak times. Always follow the National Weather Service / National Water Prediction Service and local officials; call 911 in an emergency.
