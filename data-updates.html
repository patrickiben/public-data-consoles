How the dashboards stay current

All 33 consoles open without a visitor API key. Public data automatically fills the calculations that those sources support. The table explains what updates, what it means and which quantities still need local information.

Read the observation or reporting date alongside every result. A fresh download may contain an older annual estimate. Scheduled checks are best effort; failed or incomplete updates retain labeled last-good data or show unavailable coverage. Automatic inputs do not make every one of the ten research dimensions numerically assessable.

Every response panel also has View impacts and sources: source-backed quantities, observation periods, calculation methods and named gaps update with the console selection. Annual rates become approximate counts only with a compatible same-year population. Future forecasts remain separate from historical context and published model exposure. Public reference tools retain their guidance and explicit unknown personal outcomes.
