# Public-data impacts and ten-parameter planning coverage

All 31 shared response panels use the same evidence rules as the native Coastal plan: show supported
public quantities, preserve source records/periods, and leave unsupported outcomes unestimated.
Use **Review impacts and source records** in a console. Selection and source refresh update the view;
local JSON export includes methods, source identities, reporting periods and input gaps.

| Consoles | Automatically populated public context | Boundary for impact/response planning |
|---|---|---|
| River Flood Propagation; River Flood Network | NOAA gauge values, future forecast maxima and conditional stage-impact statements; USGS/NOAA records and observed time-series diagnostics | Units, datums and forecast validity remain gauge-specific. Gauge order cannot replace population weights. Only inspected links establish connectivity; no parcel damage, routed arrival or affected-resident count is invented. |
| Quake Consequence | Selected USGS event; official PAGER modeled population bands and validated loss-probability bins; population-composition and declared ordinal band-index summaries | Exposure is modeled, losses are distributions rather than confirmed tolls. Nearest-asset dependency assumptions cannot establish real connectivity, frontier risk or leakage. |
| U.S. Local Hazards | Selected-location official warning, river, air, earthquake and weather records where current coverage is available | Expired, test and canceled notices cannot imply a current warning; missing feeds do not imply no hazard. Public point/regional observations do not predict personal losses. |
| U.S. Grid Strain | Individual EIA balancing-authority demand, matched-hour forecast benchmark and signed interchange; complete-window demand comparisons | The elapsed-hour forecast is context. Different report hours are not pooled; MW is not future MWh, unavailable supply, customers affected or an outage forecast. |
| Resilience Telemetry | Dated values from 19 public FRED series beside the existing descriptive return-network model | Observation dates differ by series. The smoothed recession-probability series is a lagged model estimate. No personal holdings, expected loss or causal transmission is inferred. |
| NHS Scotland Strain | Reported Type-1 site attendances and long waits, delayed-discharge census counts, quarterly bed occupancy and residence-to-treatment stays | Periods and populations remain separate. Long-wait groups overlap; repeat stays are not unique people or transfers. Historical occupancy is not current staffed capacity. |
| U.S. Respiratory Surveillance; U.S. Overdose Surveillance | CDC reported rates/counts, actual reporting periods and matching-period jurisdiction comparisons | Reporting coverage and provisional status remain explicit; no future health-loss or intervention-benefit model is fitted. |
| Twelve World Bank consoles: Global Health Atlas, HIV/TB/Malaria, NCD Burden, Mental Health, Nutrition, Immunization, Maternal/Child Health, Demographic Transition, Health Systems, Road Safety, Air Pollution, WASH | Published selected-country indicators. Where definitions permit: approximate annual TB episodes, crude road/suicide deaths, age-group populations, water/sanitation/clean-fuel service gaps, and source-defined workforce/bed inventories using exact-year resident populations | No neighboring-year substitution. Age-standardized mortality, birth/cohort rates, at-risk populations, lifetime risks and censored nutrition estimates retain their original units. Inventory is not available staff/beds. Overlapping quantities are not summed. |
| Global Cancer; Global NTDs; Antimicrobial Resistance | Dated IARC/WHO/GRAM and other cited published model/report estimates, with record-specific units and scope | Historical burden is not a live registry, unique-patient total, clinical prognosis or causal benefit. Incidence and mortality are not divided to infer survival. |
| Global Outbreak Tracker | Source-covered country reports and their reported periods | Cumulative counts are not active cases or present transmission; curated historical watch context remains dated. |
| USAID Consequence | USAspending agency fiscal-year resources, obligations/outlays and current public fiscal-period status | All-null future placeholder years are excluded; valid fiscal records retain dates. Agency finances do not establish a country/program cut, beneficiary loss or mortality effect. |
| Prescription Savings; Medical Cost Abroad; Medical Debt Assistant; Human Trafficking | Dated official methodology/guidance and public dataset-release metadata where available | No private query, medicine, bill, travel choice or personal situation is copied into the response plan/export. Individual savings, clinical outcomes, debt relief and safeguarding outcomes remain unknown. |
| VA Strain | Official public care-pathway guidance alongside the explicitly labeled synthetic capacity drill | Public guidance does not supply real-time census, staff or acute-care availability. Private operational overrides remain excluded. |
| Community Resilience (native plan) | FEMA NRI county and tract expected annual loss with ratings, OpenFEMA declarations, active NWS alerts, live NOAA/USGS/NIFC/AirNow feeds, dated federal lifeline inventories and Census/CDC SVI context for the searched place; St. Petersburg adds the Coastal console's published station register | Expected annual loss is a long-term average, not a forecast. Inventories are records, not operating condition. Communications, substations and hospital capacity have no keyless national source and stay unavailable. No other place's figures are substituted; free energy and network leakage remain unknown. |

The canonical ten dimensions stay visible. A dimension is assessed only when its declared inputs and
method apply; there is no requirement to fill all ten. **Review**, **Context · condition not met**, and
**Unknown** are descriptive checklist states, not official operating thresholds or safety clearances.
Public and operator actions retain separate responsibilities and escalation steps. No dispatch,
evacuation, clinical treatment, electrical switching or financial trade is authorized by these proxies.

Forecast expiry, missing evidence, invalid units, mismatched periods/denominators, duplicate record
identities and provider failures are covered by automated checks. Numeric zero is preserved when it
is a real supported observation. Unknown conclusions are withheld from both display and export.

See the [field guide](guide.html), [integration contract](CONTRACT.md), and
[automatic update guide](../data-updates.html). Public-data and offline fixtures exercise all 33 actual
entrypoints, including the unchanged native Coastal implementation and the native Community Resilience plan.

## Priority and escalation layer

All 31 shared consoles derive audience-specific ordered actions from the same public evidence as
their impacts. The native Coastal and Community Resilience checklists remain separate. Ranking uses source-specific meaning:
NOAA categories and valid forecasts, actual unexpired NWS notices, same-hour EIA reports, same-week
NHS service counts, matched-year health service gaps, and dated fiscal/reference records. A source
check comes first when applicable data is missing. Scenario inputs stay practice.

Each card names its target, evidence reason, timing, next step, escalation condition/recipient and
recheck point. Historical records support planning, not current incident activation. Sources and
periods remain in print/export. Core validity guards and domain fixtures cover selection changes,
ranking changes, expiry, stale/duplicate/missing evidence, both audiences, and all 33 offline pages.
